# Battle Scissors: Scissors and Towers

## Scissors
- **Dagger Shears**: long engraved blades that reach deep into the road; the balanced default.
- **War Nippers**: short crescent jaws that bite hard and open fast; get close.
- **Split Enders**: opens wide enough to take a whole crowd, but cuts light.
- **Scrap Snippers**: long, heavy blades with a narrow bite; aim true.
- **Ratchet Pruners**: a hooked jaw holds while one heavy blade bites down; big hits up close.
- **Cigar Cutter**: no swinging blades; fit enemies in the round hole and slam it shut.

## Towers (Pins)
- **Needle Pin** (90 thread): shoots a sewing needle at one enemy in range, then reloads.
- **Ice Pin** (100 thread): slows enemies in its ring and lets snips through Brute armor.
- **Fire Pin** (110 thread): sets passing enemies on fire; they keep burning briefly after leaving.
- **Magnet Pin** (120 thread): every few seconds pulls nearby enemies into a clump for a multi-snip.
