# Battle Scissors: Scissors and Towers

## Scissors
- **Safety Firsts** (held from the start): round-tipped kindergarten scissors; the weakest pair, and everyone's first.
- **Dagger Shears** (clear Level 4): long engraved blades that reach deep into the road; the balanced all-rounder.
- **War Nippers** (clear Level 8): short crescent jaws that bite hard and open fast; get close.
- **Split Enders** (Shop, 200 Buttons, after Level 2): opens wide enough to take a whole crowd, but cuts light.
- **Scrap Snippers** (Shop, 300, after Level 6): long, heavy blades with a narrow bite; aim true.
- **Ratchet Pruners** (Shop, 400, after Level 9): a hooked jaw holds while one heavy blade bites down; big hits up close.
- **Cigar Cutter** (Shop, 500, after Level 10): no swinging blades; fit enemies in the round hole and slam it shut.

## Towers (Pins)
- **Needle Pin** (90 thread): shoots a sewing needle at one enemy in range, then reloads.
- **Ice Pin** (100 thread): slows enemies in its ring and lets snips through Brute armor.
- **Fire Pin** (110 thread): sets passing enemies on fire; they keep burning briefly after leaving.
- **Magnet Pin** (120 thread): every few seconds pulls nearby enemies into a clump for a multi-snip.
